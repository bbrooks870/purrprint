Questions

Which printers work with PurrPrint?

Most mini “cat” thermal printers that normally use the iPrint, Tiny Print or Fun Print apps — e.g. SC03h, GB01, GB02, GB03, GT01, MX05–MX10, YT01, X5/X6 and many unbranded 58 mm Bluetooth printers. MXW01 printers use a different protocol and are not supported yet.

Which browsers work?

Chrome, Edge, Opera and Samsung Internet on Windows, macOS, ChromeOS, Linux and Android. Safari and Firefox don't support Web Bluetooth — on iPhone/iPad use the Bluefy browser.

Is PurrPrint an alternative to the iPrint, Tiny Print or Fun Print app?

Yes. PurrPrint talks to the same printers directly from your browser, with no app install, no account and no ads.

My print is too light.

Choose “Extra dark”, or open Advanced and lower the print speed. Charging the printer also helps a lot.

The printer won't connect.

Close the phone app that came with the printer (it can only talk to one device at a time), switch the printer off and on, then try again.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser and sent straight to the printer over Bluetooth.
